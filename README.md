# Little One Diary

A baby diary app for Android: feeding alarm with a baby voice, sleep watch (says "బేబీ బజ్జో ఉంది, అరవకండి" when it's loud, tells the likely reason when she cries), Krishna lullabies and mantras, memories, vaccines, notes, growth and her jataka patrika.

## Get the app
Open **Releases** on this page from your Android phone, download `LittleOne.apk`, and tap Install.

Every push to `main` builds a new APK automatically (Actions tab).
